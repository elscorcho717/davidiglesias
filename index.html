About

Product leader.
Creative thinker.
Technologist.

I'm a results-driven Product Manager with deep expertise in construction technology, AI computer vision, BIM, IoT, and digital twins — passionate about turning industry complexity into elegant, scalable software.

My path here is unconventional: I graduated Cum Laude from Berklee College of Music, spent years as a music educator and technology consultant, and ultimately found my home at the intersection of creative thinking and engineering rigor. That background shapes how I build products — with empathy, precision, and a bias toward simplicity.

Before I specialized in product management I've worked as an English/Spanish interpreter, an executive public speaking coach, a voice actor, and a private music instructor. I believe that communicating ideas — clearly, compellingly — is as important as having them.
